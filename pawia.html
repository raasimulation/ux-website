    What was the goal?
    Enabling safe and comfortable travel with pets through luggage tracking integrations with Turkish Airlines. As the goal of the Turkish Airlines Hackathon, we needed to create a feasable solution that would be implemented in the new Istanbul airport.

    Scope of the project

    Within the techincal restrictions of making a project for an already existing ecosystem, we needed to adapt to how we can make sure both parties, the customer (pets and owners) and the provider (airline) communicate on longer flights. During the 1 week duration, we did User Research on existing TA customers with pets, Brainstorming sessions to synthesize the results and Flow trees to asses possible points of intervention.

    Solution

    Giving the user the ability to see, view their pet was one of the ways of making sure that both the pet remained safe, relaxed, and the owner had the sight to take necessary action and be updated on any change that might happen during the flight. Along with this tracking feature, the user could upload much-needed documents that create a bureactratical obstacle for most owners.
